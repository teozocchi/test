## Open questions

Roughly in order of how much they unblock.

1. **Product #1.** Which product, or at least which category (water, air, heat/cold, light, sleep)? This drives almost everything else.
2. **Is a shower filter the right first product?** See [go-to-market](docs/03-go-to-market.md#is-shower-chlorine-a-problem-worth-solving--open).
3. **What does the "heavy-steel reactor" do?** Mechanism, flow rate and capacity come before industrial design.
4. **Customer.** The engineer-skeptic who already agrees, or the mainstream wellness buyer we want to win over? (The brand must appeal to the mainstream; the question is who we win *first*.)
5. **Market.** EU-wide, or one country first?
6. **Tone.** Combative, or calm and clinical?
7. **Name.** Trademark check for "First Principle" (EUIPO).
8. **Slogan.** See candidates in [philosophy](docs/01-philosophy.md#slogan--open).
9. **Business model.** Hardware only, or hardware plus consumables (cartridges)?
10. **Framework.** Astro + Shopify, or Shopify alone at launch?
11. **The €80B figure.** Needs a source before it's used anywhere.
